Samson Soyebi Foundation

Giving back to the communities that make us

The Samson Soyebi Foundation is the corporate social responsibility arm of Honeytreat Group. It was set up to make sure our growth translates into real change for people who need it most.

The foundation focuses on food security, education and care for vulnerable groups, working directly with families, schools and care homes.

  • Direct support, delivered by our own teams and volunteers
  • Long-term programmes, not one-off events
  • Free trade skills through Honeytreat Trade Academy
Our programmes

Where the foundation focuses

Four ongoing programmes address everyday needs: food, education and care.

Feed A Friend

Food support for individuals and families facing hardship in our host communities.

Student Scholarship Initiative

Scholarships that help promising students from low-income families stay in school and complete their education.

Orphanage & Elderly Homes Feeding

Regular food and supplies for orphanages and homes for the elderly.

Catering for Widows

Practical care and provisions for widows and their households.
