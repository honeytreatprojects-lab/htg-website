Our story

From one construction company to a group that builds people as well as projects

We started in 2009 as a construction company focused on quality and reliability. As we grew, we saw that the industry needed more skilled people, so we created a training academy.

Over time we expanded into equipment leasing, real estate, mining, energy and software development, building a group that serves different industries with one goal: growth and impact.

Today Honeytreat Group is a trusted name across Nigeria's construction and industrial landscape, with companies that create value for businesses, individuals and communities.

  1. 2009

    Founded in Lagos

    Honeytreat begins as a construction company with a focus on quality and reliability.

  2. Growth

    Training academy launched

    Honeytreat Trade Academy opens to close the skills gap in construction trades.

  3. Expansion

    New sectors

    Honeytreat Global Services adds equipment leasing, real estate, mining and CNG retail.

  4. Innovation

    Software development

    The group moves into technology, building platforms such as QwikCare and SurDrive.

  5. Today

    A multi-industry group

    Three operating companies, a foundation and more than 7,000 professionals trained.

Software development
